# Zentropy Labs

Order out of entropy. An independent research lab building evidence-first AI
tools, creative engines, and verification surfaces, then leaving usable artifacts
behind.

**zentropyLabs** publishes the work. **Project Telos** is the mission that maps
it. **Flywheel** is the product: route across any model, local or hosted, verify
the result, and keep a re-checkable receipt. Each tool carries its own status and
its own evidence.

## Start here

- [**Project Telos**](https://harperz9.github.io/) — the public map of engines, demos, papers, and evidence
- [**Flywheel**](https://github.com/HarperZ9/flywheel) — a companion for every model, with receipts
- [**gather**](https://github.com/HarperZ9/gather) · [**crucible**](https://github.com/HarperZ9/crucible) · [**forum**](https://github.com/HarperZ9/forum) · [**emet**](https://github.com/HarperZ9/emet) · [**buildlang**](https://github.com/HarperZ9/buildlang)

## The habit

Map the system, make the surface, test the claim, leave a usable artifact.
Honest nulls stay visible. Color only ever means a verdict. The spectrum lives in
the generative art, never in the chrome.
